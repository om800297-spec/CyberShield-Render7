LEARNING CENTER

Spot the warning signs

CyberShield combines detection with simple safety education.

01

Payment pressure

Unexpected registration, processing or verification fees deserve independent verification.

02

Urgency

Messages demanding immediate action can be designed to reduce careful verification.

03

Sensitive data

Do not share OTPs, passwords, PINs or unnecessary financial information through unsolicited messages.

04

Unrealistic offers

Guaranteed jobs, prizes or unusually high rewards can be warning signals when paired with pressure.

05

Verify independently

Use an official website or known contact method rather than the contact details inside a suspicious message.

06

Pause before clicking

Inspect links and avoid entering credentials into pages reached from unexpected messages.
